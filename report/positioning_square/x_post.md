## Alt texts

**Image 1.** Title: An alert, a framework, a measure. Three works on AI bias: the RTS article, UN report A/HRC/56/68 and this study. Three columns. The alert, RTS: a viral test, ice-breakers for an Italian, "keep your distance" for an Algerian; no count of prompts, no control; August 2026. The framework, UN: report A/HRC/56/68, how AI feeds racial discrimination, what to require; a synthesis, no test of its own; June 2024. The measure, this study: the same question, 51,975 times, to 7 open-weight AIs, only his nationality changes; 24 nationalities and a control; November 2026. Footer: the study, free, doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24146738, CC BY 4.0.

**Image 2.** Title: Of 15 questions on AI bias, the study measures 13. Three stacked bars, one per work, over the same 15 questions. RTS: 6 illustrated, 1 stated, 8 not covered. UN: 13 stated, 2 not covered. This study: 13 measured, 2 illustrated. Below: the UN raises 13 of them; among them, is technology neutral, national origin, minors, North and South, tests before deployment, audits. Footer: doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24146738.

**Image 3.** Title: The UN asks for tests. Here is one. Four UN recommendations, each with the study's answer. §68 d, test before deployment: a protocol, change one word, the nationality, and compare with "a man". §68 m, audit over time: rerun it on free Kaggle GPUs with public Kaggle notebooks; each new model joins the seven. §68 k, disaggregated data: rates by nationality, situation and age, every answer published. §68 f, transparency: open models, open data, a judge that quotes before it labels. Footer: doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24146738.

**Image 4.** Title: Name any nationality: prejudice jumps. 3 % with "a man", no nationality, an arrow, 62 % as soon as he has any nationality. Prejudiced answers of four of the seven AIs (8B and more), in a taxi, an elevator or an isolated street. Neither the RTS article nor the UN report gives such a figure. Sources: RTS, 26 August 2026; UN A/HRC/56/68. The study, free: doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24146738, CC BY 4.0.
